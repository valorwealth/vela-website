About

What Vela does

Vela is a voice-first AI assistant built for people who want less friction in their day-to-day. Instead of switching between a dozen apps to book a flight, track a meal, schedule a reminder, or check in on a goal, you simply tell Vela — and she handles it.

Vela connects the threads of daily life that usually live in separate places: your calendar, your health and nutrition, your travel plans, your bills, and the people who matter to you. She remembers context across conversations, so the more you use her, the better she understands what you actually need.
