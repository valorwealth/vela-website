Common questions

How do I delete my data?
Open Vela, go to Settings → What Vela Knows, and tap "Forget Everything" to permanently remove everything Vela has stored about you. You can also delete individual memories one at a time from the same screen.

How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions to manage or cancel at any time.

Is my data secure?
Yes. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how your information is stored and protected.
