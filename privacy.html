1. Introduction

Vela is a voice-driven personal assistant that helps you manage your schedule, bills, reminders, travel, and other daily responsibilities. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Vela is operated by Vela Tech LLC ("Vela," "we," "us," "our"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at jermaine@velalife.app.

By creating an account and using Vela, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Who can use Vela

Vela is intended for use by adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact jermaine@velalife.app.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide directly

Account information. Your name, email address, and authentication credentials when you create an account.

Voice recordings and messages. When you speak to Vela, we record the audio and convert it to text. When you type to Vela, we collect the text. Your conversations with Vela are stored in your account so Vela can maintain context.

Task and productivity information. Reminders, notes, goals, saved places, trips, and any other information you ask Vela to track.

Health information you choose to log. If you use Vela's health features, we store what you enter, which may include blood pressure readings, pulse, sleep hours, steps, energy and stress levels, mood, hydration, weight, workouts, exercise duration, intensity, and calories burned, along with any notes you add.

Vela does not read data from Apple Health or any other health platform. We only store health information you enter yourself.

Financial information you provide. Bills and due dates, investment holdings you choose to track, and travel or booking preferences.

Traveler and booking details. When you book travel through Vela, we collect the information required to complete the booking, which may include passenger names, dates of birth, contact details, and travel document information.

3.3 Information collected from services you connect

Bank and transaction information (via Plaid). If you choose to connect a bank account, our partner Plaid handles the connection. We receive and store an access token for the connection and transaction details including merchant, amount, date, and category. We never see or store your bank login credentials.

Payment card information (via Stripe). If you ask Vela to pay a bill or save a card for a booking, our payment processor Stripe handles and stores the card details. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.

Subscriptions (via Apple and RevenueCat). If you subscribe to Vela Plus, Apple processes the payment through your Apple ID, and we never receive your payment card details. Our subscription partner RevenueCat receives your purchase history and subscription status so we can unlock the features you paid for. We store your plan and your daily usage counts so we can apply plan limits.

Other connected services. If you connect a third-party service such as a task manager, we receive the data needed for that integration to work.

3.4 Information collected automatically

Basic technical and usage information such as app version, device type, error and crash reports, and timestamps of activity, used to keep the app working and to diagnose problems. Crash and error reports are collected through Sentry.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Understand and carry out your requests
  • Give Vela the context she needs to be useful, such as knowing your schedule or which bills are due
  • Perform the tasks you ask for, including searching, booking, paying bills, and setting reminders
  • Send notifications and alerts you have asked for
  • Maintain and improve the app, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse
  • Communicate with you about your account and about service changes
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your personal information for advertising.

5. How Vela uses AI, and what that means for your data

This section describes something important about how Vela works, and we want to be clear about it.

Vela's understanding and responses are powered by AI services operated by other companies. To answer you well, Vela sends relevant context from your account along with your request. Depending on what you have stored in Vela, this context may include:

  • Recent conversation history
  • Upcoming calendar events
  • Bills due soon and their amounts
  • Investment holdings and their values
  • Health entries you have logged, such as recent sleep, energy, and stress
  • People you have asked Vela to follow up with
  • Your general location and nearby saved places

This context is sent with your requests so that Vela can answer them in the situation you're actually in, rather than in the abstract.

The AI providers we use:

  • Anthropic (Claude) processes your requests and the context described above to generate Vela's responses. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its business API to train its models, and retains it only for a limited period.
  • OpenAI transcribes your voice recordings into text. OpenAI does not use data submitted through its business API to train its models.
  • ElevenLabs converts Vela's text responses into spoken audio. Only the text to be spoken is sent.

If you prefer that certain information never be sent to an AI provider, do not store that information in Vela.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active.

When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days. After that period, your conversations, health entries, financial records, contacts, and other stored information are permanently removed from our systems.

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing jermaine@velalife.app. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings to stop future charges.

Some information may persist longer where we are legally required to retain it, or in backups that are overwritten on a regular cycle. Information already shared with a third-party provider is subject to that provider's own retention practices.

8. Your rights and choices

You can:

To make a request, email jermaine@velalife.app. We will respond within 30 days.

We honor these rights for all users regardless of location. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

9. Security

We protect your information using encryption in transit, encrypted storage, authenticated access controls, and access restrictions limiting who can view user data. Bank credentials are handled by Plaid and never reach our systems. Full payment card numbers are handled by Stripe and never reach our systems.

No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you share information with us at your own risk. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.
